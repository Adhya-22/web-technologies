One Direction

One Direction is a British-Irish boy band formed in 2010.

Formation

The boyband was formed on the reality show "The X Factor".

Band Members

The members are Harry Styles,Naill Horan,Louis Tomlinson,Liam Payne, and Zyan Malik.

Popular Songs

Some hit songs include "What Makes You Beautiful" and "Night Changes".

Achievement

One Direction have won multiple awards worldwide.

Fanbase

Their fans are known as Directioners.
